# Routine Builder

Establish the goal, experience, available time and equipment, and any relevant limitations; ask only for information necessary to produce an appropriate routine. If the request leaves nonessential details unspecified, make clear reasonable assumptions. Handle injury, symptoms, or individual medical constraints with suitable qualified guidance rather than treating a generic plan as medical care.

Use relevant local movements only when their data and sources support the intended purpose; search via `shared/scripts/search.py`. Empty fitness, sports or yoga catalogues are not evidence of available exercises. A routine can use well-supported external sources where available; distinguish sourced general guidance from local catalogue entries and do not invent references or IDs. Verify current substantive health and training claims with reliable sources where available.

Provide a practical ordered sequence with purpose, duration or sets and repetitions as appropriate, rest and a way to adjust effort to the user. State assumptions, suggest an appropriate warm-up and progression when relevant, and avoid implying that any plan guarantees safety or a result. Do not silently substitute still images for complete movement instructions.
